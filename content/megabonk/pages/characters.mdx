A character in Megabonk is a starting weapon plus a passive that grows while you level. The Steam pitch says “20 Characters with unique abilities.” The store asset timestamp on that page is 14 Oct 2025. The December 2025 Spooky patch announced a new character, and the two rosters below both list 21 names. Buy from the Unlocks tab after the task is done. The menu is the live list.

<Callout kind="warn" title="Numbers that do not match">
Fox’s Luck per level, Calcium’s speed passive, Athena’s unlock wording, Amog’s poison passive, Noelle’s size and freeze bonuses, Dicehead’s level-up effect, Spaceman’s XP bonus, and Vlad’s base lifesteal are printed differently on the two wikis. Those figures are not in the table. They may change with patches.
</Callout>

## Starters

| Character | Unlock | Starts with | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fox | Available immediately | Firestaff | Luck each level, so later offers skew better. Wikis disagree on the percent. |
| Sir Oofie | Available immediately | Sword | +1% Armor per level. The passive name is Reinforced on one wiki and Fortified on the other. |

## How to use the table

- If a row says “one index also wants…”, do that extra step if the Unlocks menu still shows a lock. The menu text beats either wiki.
- Desert characters assume you can select Desert. That map is the CL4NK trip described on [Maps](/maps).
- Starting weapons still have to be in your pool. If Roberto’s Scythe or Ninja’s Katana is a separate purchase, buy the weapon before you expect the character to matter.
- Strengths are the reason to pick them, not a tier. Opinion routes are on [Builds](/builds).

Hats are not characters. A 25 Jan 2026 Steam post for v1.0.64 said it added 21 hats. This page does not list them.
