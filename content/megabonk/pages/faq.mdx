## About the numbers

SteamCharts lists an all-time peak of 116,969 concurrent players in October 2025. SteamDB has printed 117,336 for 5 Oct 2025. Both are “the launch month peak,” and they are not the same integer. Live counts in October 2026 were in the low thousands and depend on which tracker you refresh. This site uses the SteamCharts peak and does not pretend a “playing now” figure stays true.

The store page on 20 Oct 2026 showed Very Positive English reviews: 93% of 58,632. The recent-30-day line was 88% of 1,150. The store API’s recommendation total was higher than the English-only count, so this FAQ does not add those into one percentage.

The API also reported 139 achievements, a US price of $9.99, developer and publisher vedinad, release 18 Sep 2025, and a soundtrack DLC plus a demo. The press kit names the developer as Ved, the contact as vedinadgames@gmail.com, and the soundtrack as by Miguel Angell. Interface languages on the API list come to 17. The visible store table marks interface, not a full-audio column.

The game’s store text is not an English-only product. This wiki is.
