Weapons are the things that hit. Tomes are the stats underneath them. Items are the third pile. Steam never publishes a weapon total. It says you unlock characters, weapons, and items, and that upgrade offers are random and vary in rarity.

<Callout kind="warn" title="Roster size">
megabonk.org lists 30 weapons and includes Scythe, which the December 2025 Spooky update’s “new weapon” lines up with. The Megabonk Wiki weapons table this site read listed 29 and did not include Scythe. Fextralife has also said 30. If your menu has a name missing here, the menu is newer than these pages.
</Callout>

## Available from the start

| Weapon | What the indexes agree it does |
| --- | --- |
| Sword | A slash through several enemies. Sir Oofie’s start, and the kill counter for Axe and Dexecutioner. |
| Flamewalker | A fire trail behind you. |
| Lightning Staff | Lightning on nearby enemies. The level-15 task for Wireless Dagger. |
| Firestaff | Exploding fireballs. Fox’s start. |
| Chunkers | Rocks that orbit you. |
| Bone | Bones that bounce. Calcium’s start. |
| Bow | Arrows that pierce. Robinette’s start. |

## Unlock, then buy

## How to read a weapon offer

- Slots are limited until quest milestones let you buy more. See [Unlocks](/unlocks).
- Quantity and projectile speed help guns and thrown weapons. They do less for a trail that is not a projectile. The tome page says Quantity does not apply to every weapon.
- Manual aim (Sniper Rifle, Shotgun) is a different game from Aura and Flamewalker. Do not take an aim weapon on a run where you wanted to watch the minimap.
- Character pairings — who starts with what — are on [Characters](/characters).
