<Callout kind="warn" title="Opinion, not a meta report">
Nothing on this page was scraped from a leaderboard. “Comfortable,” “specialist,” and “later” are labels for how demanding the unlock is and how much the passive asks you to do on purpose. A planner or a fresh patch can make any row wrong. May change with patches.
</Callout>

Interactive build sites will tell you a full slot list for today’s version. Use those if you want a calculator. Use this if you want to know which direction is worth learning.

## Comfortable — learn the game here

| Pick | Why this is the opinion | What to take when it is offered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sir Oofie, Sword, then Aegis | Armor per level is the most readable defense in the starter pair. Aegis is his own unlock (block damage with Armor), so the weapon is the same homework as the character. | Armor, Health or Shield, Size. Cooldown once you are not dying. |
| Fox, Firestaff | Luck each level is a quiet scaling stat. You are not managing a gimmick. You are taking better offers later in the same run. | Damage, Cooldown, and Luck once you own the tome. The Luck tome itself is a rare drop, not Fox’s passive. |
| Megachad, Aura | Aura is “stand near them.” Flex is a panic button that also adds damage. The unlock is only Damage Tome level 7, which you will do anyway. | Size and Cooldown, so the circle and the flex are up. |

## Specialist — the passive is the build

| Pick | Why this is the opinion | What to take when it is offered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CL4NK, Revolver | +1% crit per level is the whole character. Precision Tome is the tome that speaks the same language, and it is also the Sniper Rifle quest. | Precision, Damage, Quantity if the revolver is counting projectiles. |
| Robinette, Bow | Gold gain per level, and damage from gold you have not spent. The opinionated part: stop opening every chest the moment you can, or you turn the passive off. | Golden Tome if you want even more gold. Damage still matters or the gold bonus has nothing to multiply. |
| Amog, Poison Flask | The unlock is absurd (50,000 poisoned with Moldy Cheese) and the passive is about poison jumping. Only bother once you like damage-over-time. | Cooldown and Duration, so the flasks keep a zone down. |
| Vlad, Blood Magic | Lifesteal per level on a weapon that can also raise max HP. A sustain run, not a first Forest clear. | Bloody Tome, which is also the weapon’s unlock task. |
| Calcium, Bone | Speed feeds the damage. You have to keep moving, which you should be doing anyway. | Agility, then Damage. Do not stand in a Charge Shrine and expect the passive to carry you. |

## Later — these make the run meaner

| Pick | Why it waits | Opinion on when to try it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sir Chadwell, Corrupted Sword | The passive adds difficulty every level. Forest Tier 3 is the unlock, so you have already done the hard map. | When a normal Tier 2 feels slow, not when you are still missing the boss timer. |
| Cursed Tome on anyone | +3.5% difficulty per level, more enemies, more XP. | After you can see a boss die with time left. It is a density tool. |
| Dicehead | 100 quests. The passive randomizes a stat or a reward, depending on which wiki you read. | When you are bored of choosing. It is a chaos run, not a plan. |
| Noelle, Birdo, Ninja, Bush | Each one asks for a stunt: microwaves, airborne tornado kills, Desert tier plus a katana drop, or a Forest duel. | Do the stunt as its own run. Do not expect the character to appear while you are trying to learn shrines. |

## What this page will not do

- It will not rank all 21 characters S through D. That number would be fake precision.
- It will not list six items per character. Item pools move, and this site is not maintaining a planner.
- It will not tell you a boss dies at a specific minute. Timers in community Graveyard guides already disagree.

If the run is dying after the build “should” work, the problem is usually the map rule, not the tome. Read [Later tiers](/late-game) before you respec a character you cannot respec.
